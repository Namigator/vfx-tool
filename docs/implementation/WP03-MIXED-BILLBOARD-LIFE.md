# Mixed point/ribbon preview and billboard life curves

The v2 preview accepts enabled `BillboardRenderer` and `RibbonRenderer` sinks in one root visual graph. `choosePreviewMode` selects `mixed`; the point compiler uses `ribbonsHandled: true` only when the path compiler is also run, and the path compiler skips billboard sinks. Both plans carry `visualOrder` from root connection order. `layerRenderOrder` combines each authored `renderOrderOffset` with that shared order. The viewport maintains point simulations and per-tick path compilation under one playback clock; scrub and restart rebuild both deterministically. Audio is still auditioned separately.

`BillboardRenderer@1` has optional `sizeOverLife` and `opacityOverLife` normalized curves. Defaults are flat 1, preserving existing documents. Curves are validated and cloned by the point compiler; the viewport compiles one sampler per layer. At each upload, normalized particle age samples the size multiplier and a per-instance opacity attribute, allowing different particles in one layer to fade independently. The point geometry is cloned per layer for that attribute and disposed with the layer. `src/render/billboardLife.ts` contains pure validation and sampling helpers.

L01 has two editable charge billboard layers sharing a point emitter at the source. Their birth sizes are 0.2 m (blue halo) and 0.04 m (pale core); life curves grow both by up to 7× and raise opacity from 0.2 to 1. A 25-tick life keeps the charge visible through the first strike tick24; tick25 shows the half-revealed bolt, tick26 the full bolt and impact, and all visual layers are gone at tick63. The 25th charge tick is deliberate overlap to avoid a blank frame at the transition.

Checks at this checkpoint: the full 417-test suite, TypeScript and production build pass. Browser inspection at desktop size showed the charge brighter/larger at tick23 than tick12, charge visible at tick24, partial bolt at tick25 without a detached impact, full bolt and target burst at tick26, and a cleared map at tick63. Full-width captures: `evidence/lightning-charge-tick23-2026-09-26.png` and `evidence/lightning-strike-tick26-2026-09-26.png`. The charge is still a single stationary point per layer; orbiting motes, moving sparks, ring/light, fade envelope of the bolt, and synchronized SFX remain future work. The in-app browser emitted no WAV download event, though the offline PCM16 mix was generated at `evidence/lightning-audio-prototype.wav`.
