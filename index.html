VFX Studio: free game VFX maker

Build spells, fire, lightning, water, ice and magic effects in your browser from 52 ready-made components and a node graph, then export them to Roblox, Unreal Engine 5 (Niagara), Unity 6 and Godot 4. Loading the editor…

Features

  • 52 components across ten elements: fire, lightning, water, ice, earth, wind, poison, shadow, light, energy.
  • Friendly knobs, colour pickers and keyframes, with a full node graph underneath.
  • Engine packages for Roblox, Unreal, Unity and Godot, plus sprite sheets, PNG sequences, GIF and MP4.
  • Layered sound with 208 CC0 samples.
